When Does a Bug Become a Sport?

A mistake happens. Someone notices it. Someone learns to repeat it. Eventually people practice it, compete with it, and build communities around it. But at what point does a bug stop being just a mistake and become a sport?

Real Examples

Wave-dashing

An unintended movement technique in Super Smash Bros. Melee became one of the most recognizable competitive skills in the game.

Accident → Discovery → Practice → Competition

Backwards Long Jump

A programming bug in Super Mario 64 eventually became one of the most famous speedrunning techniques.

Bug → Technique → Strategy → Speedrun

Bunny Hopping

An unexpected movement mechanic evolved into a skill that players spent years learning and mastering.

Movement → Skill → Community → Competition
What to notice

None of these started as sports. People discovered them, practiced them, and eventually built communities around them.
